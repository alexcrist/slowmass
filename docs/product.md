# Product contract

Status: core MVP and account deletion are implemented. See the implementation plan for verification, deployment, and remaining operational work.

## Experience

A mobile-first place to share single photos with friends. The feed is reverse chronological, finite per page, and contains the viewer’s own posts and posts from authors they currently follow through an accepted request. There is no recommendation algorithm or engagement ranking.

- Sign in through a one-time email link that confirms email ownership. No passwords or social login. New members give only a name (it need not be unique); a unique username is generated and can be changed in Edit profile once they have a profile photo. Until they add a profile photo, members use only events, Activity, and Settings (see Events); the photo unlocks posting, following, and the feed. Members without a photo are hidden from member profiles, discovery, and connection lists. Choosing a file opens a crop editor with touch dragging and pinch-to-zoom, plus keyboard and slider controls; confirming the crop immediately saves it. Later updates replace the photo rather than removing it.
- Every member after the configured first member joins by signing in through an event’s URL from an active member with a profile photo; texting that URL is the only way to invite someone. Preserve who invited whom and when permanently, including minimal tombstones through deletion: an event-link join records the event creator as inviter, the event, and the exact link version used. Joining rechecks the exact link version, the event (published, not cancelled, not over), and the creator’s eligibility; failures never fall back to another inviter. Existing members sign in without changing attribution. Email addresses remain private. Email invitations and personal or shared invite links were removed on October 17, 2026; their records remain for provenance and outstanding ones were revoked (migration 0030).
- Usernames can be changed in Edit profile. Changing one releases the old name, changes the profile link, and preserves content and relationships by account ID. No old-name redirects are retained.
- Each account has an avatar and plain-text bio. Signed-in, unblocked members can view a profile's username, name, avatar, and their follow-request state. Bios, posts, and accepted Following/Followers lists require ownership or a current accepted follow. Pending requests and blocked-account lists remain owner-only. Connection lists hide inactive, unfinished, and viewer-blocked accounts. Anonymous profile URLs reveal no profile data. Profiles have an identity header and a paginated photo grid. Your own profile and profiles you follow include Posts, Following, and Followers tabs. Your own profile also has a Settings gear at the top right; account administration and installation help live in Settings, also accessible from the desktop sidebar.
- Members who joined through a former personal email invitation follow their inviter automatically once both have profiles; subsequent unfollowing or blocking is respected, and edits and sign-ins never restore a relationship. Event links never create automatic follows; private posts still require normal accepted follow requests.
- Find people by name or username with case-insensitive fuzzy matching (letters in order, allowing skipped letters), a three-character minimum, and a 350 ms debounce. An empty search shows a shuffled member directory with explicit pages and stable ordering during pagination. Blocked and inactive members are excluded. Searches are limited to 30 per member per minute.
- Send, cancel, accept, or deny follow requests. Unfollow, remove a follower, and block an account, each requiring confirmation before changing the relationship. On another member’s profile, Unfollow and Block account live in the overflow menu. Blocking removes relationships and pending requests in both directions and prevents new interactions. Unblocking does not restore follows.
- Publish exactly one still photo with an optional plain-text caption. The composer currently omits the photo-description field; existing stored descriptions remain accessible to screen readers. Crop before publishing with a freeform rectangle, movable edges/corners, and keyboard controls; preserve the chosen aspect ratio. Show a clear unsupported-format message when conversion cannot be performed.
- Show own published posts in the home feed alongside accepted follows, using the same chronological order and pagination. Own posts also remain on the profile, which opens after posting. Do not create a synthetic self-follow.
- Feed cards show the author's username and avatar, linking to their profile. Like/unlike and comment controls use accessible icons and positive counts. Clicking the like count opens a paginated modal of members who liked the post. Comments are flat and paginated, with avatars, usernames, compact relative times, and their own likes and likers modal; there are no replies. Authors can delete their comments; post owners can delete comments on their posts. The trash icon opens a confirmation before deletion. Owners can edit captions or delete posts through a top-right three-dot menu in feed and focused views. Images and publication times cannot be edited.
- Opening a photo from the feed, a profile grid, or Activity navigates to its own post page with comments expanded. On desktop screens where a focused photo does not fill the screen width, tapping or clicking the photo opens a larger image-only viewer; full-width mobile photos have no enlargement action. A contextual back link returns to the originating list page; direct links fall back to Feed. Comment fields use a pill-shaped composer with a circular send button and automatically growing multiline input.
- Show in-app notifications for invitees joining, follow requests, new followers, likes, comments, and event activity, with safe links. Acceptance replaces a request with new-follower activity; invitation follows notify both people once both have profile photos. Existing accepted follows are not replayed. Notify the inviter (the event creator, for event-link joins) once the new member adds a profile photo. The whole joined row includes their private avatar and links to their current profile. No historical join events are backfilled; current activity visibility respects blocking, deletion and incomplete profiles. New-follower rows open the current profile and disappear if that relationship is removed. A numbered Activity badge counts unread items across all types. An item becomes read after its summary is at least half visible for 250 continuous milliseconds in the active tab; opening Activity alone does not clear unseen items. Read state is independent of follow-request decisions, so seen pending requests do not keep the badge lit. There is no manual mark-as-read action. Group consecutive likes on a post into one item; other activity in between starts a new item, which reads “N more people like your photo.” Display authoritative current counts. Like groups from before October 17, 2026 stay as they were. Activity is one newest-first, paginated timeline, with requests accepted or denied in place. Like/comment rows include a post thumbnail and open the post when selected. Web Push is off by default and can be enabled or disabled per device in Settings or from the device-setup suggestion. It uses these same activity identities for invitees joining, requests, new followers, and comments; likes never send push alerts. Receiving an alert does not mark it read. Alerts use generic lock-screen text and open the matching Activity item. Signing out stops new pushes. No SMS or email activity digests.
- Use explicit Newer/Older page navigation, preserve navigation state, and show a short horizontal line with an accessible label at the end of posts. Load images near the viewport without automatically fetching another page.
- Provide logout and account deletion. Deletion immediately removes visibility and schedules retryable object cleanup. Document backup retention separately from live deletion.

## Privacy semantics

Access is evaluated at request time. A removed follower cannot fetch more posts, bios, connection lists, or post media, including through an old image URL. Content already downloaded cannot be recalled. Blocking also hides the blocked account's profile, avatar, and interactions, and notification rendering rechecks current access. Avatars require active membership and no block in either direction; an accepted follow is not required.

In-app actions need friendly errors and retry behavior. Losing a connection during publication must not create duplicate posts or lose the ability to determine whether publication succeeded.

## Deferred

Native app-store releases, public profiles, contact import, videos, animated images, albums, stories, reels, DMs, hashtags, location features, filters, resharing, recommendations, ads, payments, email activity notifications, and offline private-photo storage.

## Product quality

Use a restrained photo-first design with a single-column feed, clear navigation, and accessible controls. Verify narrow phones through desktop layouts, keyboard and touch flows, reduced motion, screen-reader labels, and slow-network states. Never depend on color alone to communicate state.

Installation guidance and in-app install prompts are limited to iPhone, iPad, and Android. Desktop uses the website without an installation flow or device-setup suggestion.

Ten-minute magic links are the only sign-in. A sign-in lasts 60 days after the app was last opened on that device. After requesting a link, returning to the app rechecks the current session without polling. Installed apps can have separate browser sessions; an optional, collapsed unused-link helper is shown only in the installed app. No email-code or password flow is introduced.

An email without an account stays on the welcome form with a message to ask a member for an event link. Only an active member, a person signing in through a working event link, or the configured first member can advance to the email-link instructions. Signed-out visitors to an event URL see only that they’re invited and an email field; nothing about the event is shown until they sign in.

New-follower Activity offers Follow back only when no outgoing request or accepted follow exists. It sends a normal follow request, requiring acceptance before private posts become visible. Members can dismiss the prompt across devices without removing or marking the notification read. Dismissal belongs to that follower event; a later new follow can offer a fresh prompt. Follow requests and new-follower items both show the member’s private avatar and inline right-side actions.

Onboarding asks only for a name. Adding a profile photo later opens the full app directly; installation and notifications are optional choices in Settings. Android can enable either independently; iPhone/iPad must open a Home Screen app before enabling push; desktop omits installation. Adding a photo never requests notification permission.

Right after adding a profile photo, phones and tablets show one device-setup suggestion at the top of Activity, styled like an activity item and counted once in the badge until seen on that device. iPhone/iPad browsers are asked to add slowmass to the Home Screen as a first step, with numbered steps in a dialog; adding it does not enable notifications by itself. The Home Screen app then asks to turn notifications on, which shows the system permission prompt. Android asks to turn notifications on, then to install the app while the browser offers it. Each step has a × dismiss control, like Follow back. Dismissing is permanent for the account on every device; blocking the permission prompt or dismissing the install prompt opened from the suggestion also counts as No. Existing members who joined before October 14, 2026 get the suggestion once; members who already finished or dismissed the former onboarding setup screen are not asked again.

## Events

Status: implemented and tested locally on October 17, 2026; not yet deployed (manual migration 0030). See the implementation plan for rollout.

Partiful-style events help friends meet up without changing the photo-only feed.

- **Contents.** One cover photo, a title, description, plain-text location (no maps, geotagging or nearby discovery), start time in the event's time zone with an optional end, hosts, a guest list, and an activity log. Guests answer Going, Maybe, or Can't go. Only members with a profile photo create events; the creator is the host (adding co-hosts is not built yet). Hosts can edit details, replace the cover or link, and cancel. Changing the time or location, and cancelling, notifies everyone who answered.
- **One URL.** Each event has one URL, `/#event/<link>`, carrying a revocable credential in the fragment (so it never reaches servers or messaging-app previews). It is what Share link sends—through the phone’s share sheet, otherwise copied—and what the address bar shows on the event page, for hosts and guests alike. Signed out, the URL shows only that you’re invited and asks for an email; nothing about the event is revealed. Signing in through it, or opening it while signed in, records the link version for that member and shows the details, cover, and host. Answering adds the guest list, answer counts, and activity log, and lasts through link replacement. Replacing the link stops it for everyone else, including unopened sign-in emails and members who opened it without answering. Answers close when the event is cancelled or over (at its end time, or twelve hours after it starts), and the link then admits no new people.
- **New people.** The URL is the invitation. Signing in through it by email creates a membership with the event creator as inviter, recording the event and link version—useful for tracing mass sign-ups to one link. New members then give only a name. Existing members signing in through it keep their attribution.
- **Members without a photo.** They see a reduced app: their events, Activity, and Settings (add a profile photo, edit name, installation, notifications, sign out, delete account). Event screens and event Activity identify people by name, not username. They stay hidden from search, the directory, and connection lists, and cannot post or follow. Adding a profile photo unlocks the full app.
- **Navigation.** The phone bottom bar is Feed, Find, New post, Events, Profile. Activity, with its unread badge, sits at the right of the wordmark header on every phone page; on your own profile it is one row above the Settings icon. The header still scrolls with the page. The desktop sidebar keeps Activity and adds Events. Members without a photo get Activity and Settings in the header on every screen size.
- **Notifications.** Activity and opt-in Web Push only, as for other activity. Hosts hear about each guest's current answer and every message; guests hear host messages, time or location changes, and cancellation. No email is sent about events, changes, or cancellations.
- **Guest privacy.** Hosts and people who answered see each other's names, and avatars when present. Blocking the creator denies the event; other blocks hide each person from the other.
- **Limits.** Ten new events per member per day, 500 answers per event, and 100 event messages per member per day.
- **Out of scope.** Co-hosts, plus-ones, capacity limits, waitlists, ticketing or payments, public or discoverable events, email or SMS reminders, and event photo albums.
