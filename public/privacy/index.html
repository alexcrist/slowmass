Your privacy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Slowmass is a personal project for sharing photos and events with friends. Your information is used to make the app work, not to make money from you.

Your data is yours. Sharing it here does not give us ownership of your photos, words, or other content. You give us permission only to store, process, and show it as needed to provide the features you choose and keep Slowmass working safely. We do not use it for unrelated purposes.

We do not feed your data to AI. We do not send your content or personal data to AI tools, use it to train models, or authorize anyone else to do that.

We never sell or rent your data, or share it with advertisers or data brokers. There are no ads or trackers that follow you across websites.

What we keep, and why

We keep your email for sign-in; the profile, photos, comments, and event details you choose to add; and your follows, invitations, and activity. This lets us show things to the right people and keep the app safe. Your email is not shown to other members.

Essential cookies keep you signed in. Browser storage remembers app preferences. We process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and error reports for security and troubleshooting. If you enable notifications, we store a delivery address for your device.

If you choose Google sign-in

Google shares your email, account identifier, and basic profile information, which can include your name and picture. We use your verified email and account identifier only to sign you in and connect you to your existing Slowmass account. We do not save Google's profile name or picture, or retain its access tokens. We do not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, Drive files, or Google Photos.

You can remove Slowmass's access in your Google Account. This does not delete your Slowmass account; you can still use an email sign-in link.

Who can see what

Your posts and bio are visible to you and followers you approve. Your name, username, and profile photo identify you to other signed-in members, subject to blocking. Comments and likes are visible to people who can see the post.

Event links can be forwarded. Someone with a link can see the event's title, date, and cover before signing in; signed-in people with access can see its details and shared activity. A shared link may also create a preview in another app. Anyone who can view something may save or share a copy.

How we protect it

We use encrypted connections, restricted photo storage, access checks, and encrypted backups. Slowmass is not end-to-end encrypted: the operator and service providers can access data when needed to run, protect, or fix the app.

Cloudflare hosts the app and its data; Resend delivers sign-in emails; Google handles Google sign-in if you choose it. Your device's notification service delivers notifications if you enable them. Private encrypted backups may be stored in Google Drive. We share information only as needed for these services, at your request, or when legally required to do so.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your profile, remove followers, block people, and turn off notifications. To leave, choose Delete account in Settings. Your account and content become unavailable in the app immediately; background cleanup removes personal data and stored photos.

We retain minimal invitation-history records to prevent abuse, without your profile or email after cleanup. Deleted data may remain in recovery copies until they expire; our encrypted-backup retention target is seven days, with manual cleanup. Copies other people saved are outside our control.

Questions or requests?

Slowmass is run by Alex Crist. Email the.blonde.hamster@gmail.com to ask about your data, request a copy or correction, or get help deleting it.

If our data practices change, we will update this page and tell you about meaningful changes. We will ask before using Google data for a new purpose. Your ownership and our promises not to sell your data or use it for AI stay.
